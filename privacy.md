# Privacy Policy — Crash Rocket

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what data the mobile game **Crash Rocket** (package
`games.crash.rocket`, the "Game") collects, why it collects it, and who it is shared with.
By installing or using the Game you agree to this policy.

## 1. Who we are

Crash Rocket is developed and published by **Crash Games** (the "we", "us"). You can reach us at
**crashgames.support@gmail.com** for any question about this policy or about the data associated with your device.

## 2. We do not ask you to register

The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no password. We do not ask for your name, postal
address, phone number, email address, photos, contacts, files, precise location, or any
government identifier. Everything below is collected automatically by the Game and by the
third-party services listed in section 4.

## 3. What is collected and why

| Data | Why it is collected | Is it linked to you? |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID) | To show advertising, to limit how often the same ad is repeated, and to measure whether an ad campaign that led you to install the Game worked | Linked to your device identifier, not to your identity |
| Device and connection information: device model, operating system version, language, country derived from IP address, screen size, network type | To render the Game correctly, to pick suitable ad formats, and to diagnose problems | Device identifier only |
| In-game activity: rounds played, multipliers reached, virtual coin balance, screens opened, buttons pressed, ads watched | To understand which parts of the Game are used, to balance difficulty, and to decide what to build next | Device identifier only |
| Crash and diagnostic reports: stack traces, memory state at the moment of a crash, Game version | To find and fix crashes | Device identifier only |
| Purchase history: which in-app product was bought and when | To deliver what you paid for and to support refund requests | Device identifier and your Google account, handled by Google Play |

We do **not** collect audio, video, photographs, contacts, calendar entries, SMS, call logs,
health data, browsing history outside the Game, or precise (GPS) location.

## 4. Third-party services

The Game contains software development kits (SDKs) from the companies below. They receive some
of the data in section 3 directly and process it under their own privacy policies:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|
| AppLovin MAX (AppLovin Corporation) and the advertising networks it mediates | Serving and measuring advertising | https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ |
| Adjust GmbH | Attributing installs to marketing campaigns, fraud prevention | https://www.adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy/ |
| Google Firebase — Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config (Google LLC) | Usage analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration of game parameters | https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy |
| Google Play Services and Google Play Billing (Google LLC) | App distribution, in-app purchases, advertising ID | https://policies.google.com/privacy |

Advertising networks mediated by AppLovin MAX may show advertisements from a wide range of
industries, including advertisements for betting and gambling services where local law and the
platform permit it. Those advertisements come from third parties; we do not operate the services
they promote, and clicking them takes you outside the Game.

## 5. Advertising and your choices

Advertising in the Game may be personalised using your Advertising ID. You control this on your
device:

- **Android**: Settings → Privacy → Ads → **Delete advertising ID** (or **Opt out of Ads
  Personalisation** on older versions). After deleting the ID, ads still appear but are no longer
  personalised.
- In regions where it is required (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland,
  and US states with comparable laws), the Game shows a consent dialog before any personalised
  advertising or analytics identifier is used. You can withdraw that consent at any time from the
  privacy link in the Game's settings screen.

## 6. Payments

The Game offers optional in-app purchases of virtual coins. Payments are processed entirely by
**Google Play**. We never see or store your card number, bank details or billing address — we
only receive confirmation from Google that a purchase succeeded, so that the coins can be
credited.

Virtual coins are a game feature with no monetary value. They cannot be withdrawn, transferred,
sold, or exchanged for money, cryptocurrency or prizes of any kind, and buying them is not a
wager or a deposit. The Game is entertainment only.

## 7. Children

The Game is not directed to children. It is rated for teenagers and older, we declare an adult
target audience on Google Play, and the Game shows an age confirmation screen on first launch.
We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age at which consent is required in their
country. If you believe a child has used the Game and you want the associated data removed,
write to **crashgames.support@gmail.com** and we will delete it.

## 8. Where data is processed and how long it is kept

The services in section 4 operate globally and may process data in the United States, the
European Union and other countries, including countries whose data-protection law differs from
yours. Transfers rely on the safeguards published by each provider (standard contractual clauses
or an equivalent mechanism).

Retention is set by each provider: analytics events are kept for up to 14 months, crash reports
for up to 90 days, and advertising identifiers for the period stated in the policies linked
above. Your progress and coin balance are stored **on your device only** and disappear when you
uninstall the Game.

## 9. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or
delete the data associated with your device, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of
these rights, write to **crashgames.support@gmail.com** with the subject "Privacy request" and include your
Advertising ID (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads) so we can locate the records. We answer within
30 days.

You can also delete everything yourself at any time: uninstalling the Game removes all local
progress, and deleting your Advertising ID on the device breaks the link between your device and
the records held by the advertising and analytics services above.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is
significant, show a notice inside the Game before the change takes effect.
