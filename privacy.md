# Privacy Policy — Crash Rocket

**Effective date:** September 3, 2026
**Developer:** Crash Games (contact: crashgames.support@gmail.com)

Crash Rocket ("the App") is a free casual arcade game for Android. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and your choices. We do not offer real-money gambling; in-game coins and fuel have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn.

## Data we collect

| Data | Purpose | Shared with |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID (Google AdID), IP address, device model, OS version, language, app version | Serving and measuring ads, fraud prevention | AppLovin (MAX mediation) and its ad-network partners, Google AdMob |
| Install source, attribution events (install, session, in-app events) | Measuring marketing campaigns | Adjust |
| Gameplay analytics (rounds played, multipliers, purchases, ad views), crash reports | Improving the game, fixing bugs, balancing | Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) |
| Purchase tokens for in-app purchases | Delivering and verifying purchases | Google Play Billing |

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. Payments are processed by Google Play.

## Advertising
The App shows ads (rewarded video, interstitial, banner) delivered through AppLovin MAX and partner networks. Ads may be personalized using your Advertising ID where you have not opted out. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Users in the EEA/UK are shown a consent dialog (Google UMP / TCF) before personalized ads.

## Children
The App is intended for users aged 16 and older (content rating Teen / PEGI 16). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data retention and deletion
Analytics and attribution data are retained for up to 24 months. To request deletion of data associated with your device, email crashgames.support@gmail.com with your Advertising ID (Android Settings → Google → Ads) or use the in-app "Delete my data" option in Settings once available.

## Security
Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Third-party SDKs process data under their own policies: [AppLovin](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/), [Adjust](https://www.adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your rights
Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD and others) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object to personalized advertising. Contact us at the email above.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
